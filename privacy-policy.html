Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Puzzle Brainly (the "App", package com.onxmariners.arrowpuzzlebrainly) handles information. The App is published by Monkey Mariners ("we", "us"), Vill- Sukar Beg Chak, P.O + P.S- Khusrupur, Patna, Bihar 803202, India. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: the game needs no account, works offline, and keeps your progress on your own phone. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob advertising SDK collects to show ads.

1. Information you give us

None. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photographs, contacts, files, microphone, camera or location. There is no sign-up, no login and no user account.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally, using Android's standard app storage. It never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

WhatWhy
Level progress and highest level unlockedSo you can continue where you stopped
Stars earned per levelTo show your rating on the level map
Hint balanceTo track how many hints you have left
Settings (theme, sound, haptics, animation)To remember how you like to play
First-open time and launch countTo show the beginner tip only to new players

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Analytics

We record anonymous gameplay events — for example that a level was started or completed, that a hint was used, or that a theme was selected — so we can balance difficulty and improve the design. These events contain no name, email, phone number, device identifier or any other information that identifies you, and they are not linked to your identity or combined with data from other sources.

4. Advertising

The App shows a small number of ads, including optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game hints or an extra heart. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob.

To select and measure ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and technical information such as device type, operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and whether an ad was shown or clicked. We never receive your advertising ID ourselves, and we cannot use it to identify you.

You control that identifier. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset it or to opt out of personalised advertising entirely. The game remains fully playable either way.

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. It contains no chat, no accounts, no user-generated content and no links out of the game other than the ad network and these policy pages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact zamefy@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Permissions the App requests

PermissionUsed for
INTERNETLoading ads only. Gameplay never needs a connection.
AD_IDAllows AdMob to read the advertising ID described above.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAdded by the ads SDK to detect whether you are online.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. We do not share data with third parties other than the advertising provider described in section 4.

8. Security

Because the App keeps your data on your own device and transmits nothing to us, there is no account of yours for us to lose. Ad requests are transmitted over encrypted connections by the Google SDK.

9. Your rights

You can delete all data the App holds about you at any moment by resetting progress or uninstalling. If you are in a region with data-protection rights such as the GDPR or the Indian DPDP Act and you wish to make a request, write to zamefy@gmail.com. Since we hold no personal data identifying you, in most cases the answer will simply be that there is nothing stored on our side.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date. Material changes will also be noted in the app listing.

Contact us

Questions about this document? Write to zamefy@gmail.com and we will reply within 7 working days.
