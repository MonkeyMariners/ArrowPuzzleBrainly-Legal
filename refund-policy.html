Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Arrow Puzzle Brainly is free to download and free to play. As of 20 September 2026 the App contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, so there is nothing to refund. This policy explains what will happen if paid items are added in a future version.

1. Free today

Every one of the 655 levels, all four themes, and every feature in the game is available at no cost. The App is funded by advertising, described in the Rewards & Earnings Policy. We will never charge you without you explicitly choosing to buy something.

2. If we add purchases later

A future version may offer optional paid items, most likely a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. If that happens:

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Prices shown in the App include applicable taxes where Google Play displays them that way.
  • Purchases unlock immediately and are tied to the Google account that bought them.

3. How to request a refund

Because Google Play is the seller of record, refunds go through Google:

  1. Within 48 hours of purchase — request it directly at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, or in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. Google usually processes these automatically.
  2. After 48 hours — write to zamefy@gmail.com with your order number (it starts with GPA.), the date, and what went wrong. We will review it and, where the request is reasonable, ask Google to refund it.

Refunds are returned to your original payment method. Google's timings apply — usually a few business days, sometimes up to two weeks for bank transfers.

4. When we will refund

SituationOutcome
You were charged twice for the same itemRefunded
You paid but the item never unlocked, and a reinstall did not fix itRefunded
A bug in our App made a paid feature unusable and we cannot fix it promptlyRefunded
An unauthorised purchase was made on your account (report promptly)Referred to Google, supported by us
You changed your mind within 48 hoursHandled by Google's standard process

5. When we normally cannot refund

  • Virtual items such as hints or hearts that have already been used up.
  • Requests made long after the purchase where the item worked as described.
  • Progress lost because the App was uninstalled or its data cleared — progress is stored only on your device and cannot be recovered.
  • Dissatisfaction with difficulty, ad frequency or the look of the game, where the item purchased worked as described. Tell us anyway — that feedback shapes updates.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing here limits the consumer rights you have under the law of your country, including any statutory right to cancel a digital purchase.
