Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This page sets out the practical, day-to-day rules for using Arrow Puzzle Brainly and this website. It sits alongside the Terms and Conditions, which is the full legal agreement. Where the two differ, the Terms and Conditions prevail.

1. Acceptable use

Use the game as it is meant to be used: to play puzzles. Do not use it to break the law, to harm anyone, or to interfere with the service other people receive.

2. Fair play

  • Do not use modified versions of the App, cheat tools, memory editors, macros or bots.
  • Do not attempt to unlock levels, hints or hearts other than by playing or by watching a rewarded video you genuinely chose to watch.

3. Your device and connection

You are responsible for your own device, its settings, and any mobile data charges incurred while ads are loaded. The puzzles themselves need no connection; you can play the entire game in aeroplane mode.

4. Health and comfort

Take a break every so often. If you experience eye strain, headaches or discomfort while playing, stop and rest. If you are sensitive to motion or flashing effects, turn off Animation & effects in the game's Settings.

Some pages link to third-party sites such as Google, GitHub and LinkedIn. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

8. Reporting a problem

If something in the game is broken, unfair or looks like a bug, tell us on the support page. Reports genuinely do change the game — difficulty and touch handling are tuned from what players report.

9. Suspension

We may withdraw your licence to use the App if you break these rules, particularly the fair-play rules in section 2.
