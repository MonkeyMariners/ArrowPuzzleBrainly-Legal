Data & Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Executive Summary: Arrow Puzzle Brainly does not require, create, or support user accounts. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, password, or cloud profile. All game progress and preferences are stored strictly on your local device. You can permanently delete 100% of your data at any moment directly from your device without needing developer intervention.

1. User Accounts Status

Arrow Puzzle Brainly has no user account system.

You play without signing up, logging in, or connecting to any social media platform. Because no central user account or cloud profile is ever created, there is no online user profile or account record hosted on our servers to deactivate or delete.

2. How to Delete All Stored Game Data

All your gameplay data — including unlocked levels (up to level 2,200), solve times, stars earned, hint balances, and sound/vibration/theme preferences — is stored locally in your phone's private app sandbox. You can permanently delete this data using any of the three methods below:

Method A: Inside the Game (Instant Self-Service)

  1. Open Arrow Puzzle Brainly on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings gear icon on the home screen.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom panel.
  4. Tap the red button labeled Reset progress.
  5. Confirm your choice on the dialog prompt.

Effect: All progress across all levels, solve records, stars, hint balances, and custom settings are instantly and permanently purged from your device storage, resetting the game back to factory condition.

Method B: Via Android Device Settings

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & Notifications / Manage Apps).
  3. Locate and tap Arrow Puzzle Brainly.
  4. Tap Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Effect: Completely erases all local database records, cache files, and preferences stored by the App.

Method C: Uninstalling the Game

Simply uninstalling Arrow Puzzle Brainly from your device triggers Android's OS-level package removal, which immediately and irreversibly deletes all sandbox data, preferences, and files associated with package com.onxmariners.arrowpuzzlebrainly.

3. Advertising Identifier Data (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may utilize your device's Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) to serve ads, limit frequency, and detect fraud.

Because advertising IDs are managed by Google Play Services rather than stored on our servers, you can reset or delete this identifier at any time through Android:

  • Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Tap Delete advertising ID to permanently remove your device's ad identifier, or tap Reset advertising ID to generate a fresh anonymous identifier.
  • Optionally, disable Personalised Ads on the same screen.

4. Requesting Deletion of Email Correspondence & Inquiries

If you have communicated with us via email (for example, for bug reports, questions, or support inquiries) and wish to have all related emails and personal contact information permanently removed from our records, you may submit a deletion request:

  • Email: Send a request to monkeymariners@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Data Deletion Request — Arrow Puzzle Brainly
  • Details to provide: The email address used to contact us.
  • Processing Time: We will process your request, permanently purge all email correspondence from our email servers within 30 days, and send you a final deletion confirmation.
