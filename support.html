Before you write

These cover most questions:

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on your device only — there is no cloud save. Uninstalling the App, clearing its data, or moving to a new phone will reset it, and we cannot restore it from our side.

A level looks impossible

It isn't. Every level is generated so that a full solution exists, and removing an arrow can only free other arrows — you can never trap yourself. Look for an arrow whose entire path to the edge of the board is empty, and use a hint if you are stuck.

The game is too hard, or too easy

Tell us which level number and what felt wrong. Difficulty is tuned from exactly this kind of report.

Too many ads / an ad broke something

Ads should appear at most once every four completed levels and never during a puzzle. If you see more than that, send us your device model and Android version — that is a bug, not the design.

A tap did not register

The board is fixed in place and every cell is a large tap target, so this should not happen. If it does, tell us the device model and, if you can, the level number.

Turning off sound, vibration or animation

All three are switches in Settings inside the game.

What to include in a bug report

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen)
  • The level number, if it is about a specific puzzle
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one
